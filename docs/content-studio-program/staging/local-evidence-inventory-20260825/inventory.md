# Local evidence inventory — 2026-08-25

## Verdict

**Yes: the existing corpus is a substantial, targeted sample of high-performing content.** It is
not merely 499 unexamined links. It contains 499 metric-bearing records across 65 accounts on 13
platforms; 132 records already have admitted analysis, and 123 of those have a recorded relative
performance comparator.

**No: it does not establish that we have the top creators or a complete cross-platform market
sample.** The corpus is a deliberate research sample, not a census or a ranked universe. “Top
creator” requires a defined comparison universe that this corpus does not contain.

The previous slate gate was therefore wrong to equate public-profile access limits with missing
local evidence. Its 54 `blocked` labels describe fresh public lookup attempts only. They do not
describe the availability or quality of these local records.

## What it can and cannot answer now

- **High-performing content:** yes, on a platform-specific basis. The corpus has metrics for all
  records, explicit source-winner provenance for 207 records, and existing relative-performance
  analysis for 123 admitted records. These are different evidence types and remain separate.
- **Useful creator samples:** yes, for 58 accounts with two or more engagement-bearing records.
  This permits bounded within-account comparison where the source and measurement context allow
  it.
- **Top creators:** no. The corpus contains selected accounts and communities, not a defined
  platform-wide or niche-wide comparison universe.
- **Niche coverage:** partial. Existing niche labels and analyses can be inventoried, but we have
  not yet produced a body-free niche-coverage table from them.
- **Format coverage:** broad but uneven. The corpus includes text-only, image, carousel,
  short-video, video, link-preview, mixed, thread, and repost-with-comment records. It cannot
  safely yield content-mechanism learning from the 169 body-incomplete or 44 body-unknown records.
- **Platform coverage:** 13 platforms are represented. Pinterest, a configured platform, has no
  corpus evidence, so it is a real gap rather than an access-blocked account.

## Corrected next step

Replace the prior all-hold candidate slate with a local-evidence-backed slate. Start from the 132
already admitted analyses and the 58 accounts with repeat engagement evidence; retain public
research only for metadata gaps that the local inventory cannot answer. No canonical data write
is authorized by this inventory.

This report intentionally contains no creator content, account ranking, or universal viral score.
