>
> I learned this goal-setting system from Raymond Aaron. He calls it "M-T-O"...
>
> ✅ M = Minimum
>
> These are your expected goals. Aka 👉🏻 the results you think you're pretty easily able to achieve.
>
> ✅ T = Target
>
> These are your binary goals. Aka 👉🏻 the goal you're really, really hoping that you'll hit. This is the only many people make and focus on...
>
> ✅ O = Outrageous
>
> These are the goals where you think a miracle would have to happen in order to reach them.
>
> Now, here's why I LOVE this goal-setting system so much...
>
> When the only goal you set is binary, you're actually LESS LIKELY to hit it.
>
> What tends to happen is that when you're only focused on one goal, you begin losing motivation, and so you stop pushing as hard (or maybe stop trying altogether)...
>
> But when you set 3 levels of goals, you gain confidence by successfully completing your "minimum" goals...
>
> Which gives you tons of momentum and helps you EASILY blow past your "target" goals...
>
> And you end up surprisingly closer to your "outrageous" goals.
