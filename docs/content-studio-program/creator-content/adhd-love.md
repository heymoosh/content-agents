# ADHD Love (Rich & Rox): content library

**Handle:** @adhd_love_ (Instagram)
**Primary platform:** Instagram
**Primary media type:** short video (Reels)
**Audience size:** 1.3M followers (at capture)
**Topic(s):** ADHD and nonlinear work
**Capture method:** Reels tab, scrolled through roughly the most recent 45 posts recording each tile's visible view count, then opened the 30 highest-view-count reels from that set individually for full detail. Because the account has 857 total posts, this is the top 30 of the most recent ~45, not a verified top 30 of all-time: a small number of older, higher-view reels outside that window may exist uncaptured.
**Posts captured:** 30/30
**On-screen text / spoken transcript method note:** Reel video playback would not load in this automated browser session (confirmed on multiple reels: `readyState` stuck at 0/`HAVE_NOTHING`, `networkState` stuck at loading, the player degrading to Instagram's own "Sorry, we're having trouble playing this video" error after any play attempt; no closed-caption text track was ever exposed on the `<video>` element). As a result, **"On-screen text (verbatim)" below is a partial capture**: exactly what's legible in the static frame(s) Instagram rendered for each reel (its grid-tile thumbnail and/or its detail-page poster frame), not a scrub through the full clip, so on-screen text appearing at other points in a video may be missing. **"Spoken transcript (verbatim)" could not be captured for any entry**: this is recorded uniformly as "Not captured" rather than "none," since "none" would falsely assert a reel has no speech.
