Note on ordering: Instagram's Reels tab is not sorted by view count, it is reverse-chronological
(with two pinned posts always shown first). We scrolled through 51 posts, recorded each one's
visible view count, and selected the top 30 by that count. This is "top 30 of the most recent 51
scanned," not a verified all-time top 30 for the account. Several of the highest-viewed posts are
collaboration/repost posts (with @melrobbins, @themelrobbinspodcast, @drmaryclaire's unPAUSED,
@cbsmornings, @lewishowes, @areallygoodcry, @bestoflaughlines) where Dr. Hamdani appears as a
guest; the permalink for these resolves to the co-author's account handle even though the post
also appears on @thepsychdoctormd's own Reels tab. The "eye" view count shown in the grid is not
displayed on individual post pages, which show likes/comments/shares instead; both are recorded
below where available. Video playback did not work in this automated browser session for any of
the 30 posts tested (confirmed on a spot check across the set), so spoken transcripts are marked
not captured throughout; on-screen text reflects only what is legible in the static poster frame
actually rendered.
