# Broad pattern-research policy — 2026-08-25

The 65 current accounts are evidence-bearing **seeds** only. They are not already viral, approved,
best, complete, ranked, or the only candidates that may be investigated. Researchers may add
candidates from the 371-account catalog or bounded public research, but all resulting research
stays in isolated noncanonical staging packages until one consolidated candidate-slate review.

Keep these independent:

1. Niche relevance
2. Broad-platform performance
3. Format-specific performance
4. Relative outperformance where a valid account baseline exists
5. Repeatability across more than one post
6. Evidence quality: traceable items, usable metrics/dates/denominators, and caveats

Do not collapse them into a score or universal ranking. Every proposed account classification must
carry identity/platform, evidence references, topic/niche, medium/formats, examples, available
measurements, baseline or explicit gap, pool rationale, caveats, confidence, and an include,
exclude, hold, or research-further recommendation. Body text remains out of the packets.

The next human gate is one consolidated candidate slate, after research completes. It will name
recommended niche, broad-platform, and format pools; held/rejected candidates; coverage and
baseline gaps; and the exact follow-up decisions required for canonical stewardship.
