Review evidence · Phase 4

Content and reader learning can reach Venture without becoming an automatic business decision.

This review shows the behavior change, the two human gates, and what the system deliberately refuses. It contains no generated private content.

Before

The handoff was a read-only readiness view. Legacy callers could omit an explicit bundle, measured Content outcomes were not mandatory, and nothing created a durable Venture-owned receipt.

Now

A handoff carries an evidence tier and honest claim ceiling. Source/evidence refs are always required; exact experiment lineage is required only for directional and controlled tiers. Decision state is append-only. Acceptance creates one internal, non-publishable Venture artifact and one canon decision.

Normal path

  1. Signals-origin learning comes from analytics or measured Content experiments. Ordinary active account-level metrics appear as engagement/attention receipts; redacted comments, replies, DMs, emails, and follow-up questions appear as qualitative/resonance receipts. Raw text and respondent hashes do not enter the evaluator.
  2. Venture-native surveys, comments, emails, and DMs enter from their existing manually judged response intake. They do not need a redundant Signals decision.
  3. Both paths retain the evidence ladder: engagement (attention), qualitative/comments (resonance), surveys (stated-need), directional (directional-comparison), controlled (bounded-comparison), funnel (behavioral-intent), and business (observed-demand).
  4. Venture evaluates the input against its current hypotheses, lead-generation ideas, products, offers, and strategy, then recommends no change, change, or test.
  5. Muxin separately chooses accept, decline, or request more evidence on that recommendation.
  6. Acceptance retains a proposal only. It does not publish, clear a checkpoint, unlock a phase, mutate configuration, select a winner, or claim demand.

Example decision packet

Hypothesis: A specific opening produces more qualified lead-magnet signups than the control opening.

Evidence example: A survey may support a stated-need ceiling; a controlled two-arm experiment may support a bounded-comparison ceiling. Neither becomes observed demand without business evidence.

Review context: Scope, treatment/control samples, plan digest, interpretation id, caveats, qualification, evidence status, named Venture and phase, and exact source/variant/experiment/content lineage remain visible at both gates.

Signals recommendation: Adopt as a bounded input for Venture example-venture, current phase. Confidence and caveats remain visible.

Venture effect after acceptance: Store the evidence-linked receipt. Learning may recommend no-change, change, or test; Muxin separately accepts, declines, or requests more evidence. A test flows through the canonical Experiment planner, normal plan approval, Content drafting/review/publishing/measurement, then back to Signals and Venture learning.

Fail-closed examples

  • Blocked: Evidence refs missing, or a claim ceiling higher than the tier permits.
  • Blocked: Directional/controlled lineage missing its exact experiment, Content, treatment, or control references.
  • Blocked: A proposal line that tries to claim adoption without a separate Signals decision event, or any automatic phase/checkpoint/configuration/winner/demand mutation.
  • Blocked: A client request that tries to supply its own authority, evidence, approval identity, or gate reference.
  • Blocked: Concurrent or repeated writes that drift from the original identity. Exact retries collapse to the same artifact.

Implementation evidence: Deterministic contract, lifecycle, tier/ceiling, tamper, acceptance, idempotency, and rules-parity tests. This artifact claims no live operational proof. “Observed demand” means attributable business evidence at the business ceiling; “winner” means an explicit, scoped, measured comparison fact with its declared sample, not an inference from any lower rung.
