Review evidence · Phase 4

Measured Content learning can reach Venture without becoming an automatic business decision.

This review shows the behavior change, the two human gates, and what the system deliberately refuses. It contains no generated private content.

Before

The handoff was a read-only readiness view. Legacy callers could omit an explicit bundle, measured Content outcomes were not mandatory, and nothing created a durable Venture-owned receipt.

Now

A handoff requires exact experiment lineage, both measured arms, an accepted Signals interpretation, and an attributed funnel or business outcome. Acceptance creates one internal, non-publishable Venture artifact and one canon decision.

Normal path

  1. Content publishes approved treatment and control variants with exact lineage.
  2. Signals reads measured outcomes, keeps attention, conversation, audience, and business families separate, and proposes a named Venture input.
  3. Muxin chooses adopt, decline, or request more evidence in Signals.
  4. Only an adopted proposal appears inside the named Venture's existing room.
  5. Muxin separately chooses accept, reject, or request more evidence in Venture.
  6. Accept writes one signals-input artifact and an append-only canon decision. It does not publish, clear a checkpoint, unlock a phase, or alter a locked business decision.

Example decision packet

Hypothesis: A specific opening produces more qualified lead-magnet signups than the control opening.

Measured evidence: Both planned variants reached the minimum sample; an attributed outcome-ledger record links the treatment to qualified funnel outcomes.

Signals recommendation: Adopt as a bounded input for Venture example-venture, current phase. Confidence and caveats remain visible.

Venture effect after acceptance: Store the evidence-linked receipt for the next Venture judgment. Do not infer demand or change the offer.

Fail-closed examples

  • Blocked: Replies, likes, impressions, follows, or provider-only clicks with no attributed outcome record.
  • Blocked: Missing control arm, wrong variant IDs, stale phase/rules, changed proposal bytes, or rejected Signals interpretation.
  • Blocked: A client request that tries to supply its own authority, evidence, approval identity, or gate reference.
  • Blocked: Concurrent or repeated writes that drift from the original identity. Exact retries collapse to the same artifact.

Implementation evidence: Focused contract, route, store, Venture acceptance, rules-parity, idempotency, crash-recovery, and concurrency tests. Full local check and browser suite are recorded in the PR.
