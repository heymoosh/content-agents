linkedin · summary

America's core promise is that anyone can pursue happiness here. In practice, that promise often gets heard as money, high status, and owning lots of things.

Accounts from people nearing death point somewhere else. The biggest regrets are usually not about money or things they could not buy. They are about living under others' expectations, leaving love un-pursued, and never letting spirit and courage live freely.

It was the biggest fear of my young adult life to end up in a career that drained me. I spent years hunting for passion so I would not live an unexamined life. Along the way I realized my most fulfilling moments had little to do with self-satisfying activities. They were moments where I felt part of something bigger: A mission toward the shared well-being of others and of society.

Happiness, at least for me, meant financial stability and work toward a freer, just, courageous world that still has not arrived. There's a term for that kind of happiness: Eudaimonia. Flourishing and living ethically, rather than chasing mere pleasure or mere ambition.

If happiness is defined as wealth, status, stuff, and self-interest, we keep justifying almost any means to that end. Community welfare, democracy, kinship, and the social fabric that makes individual freedom possible all become expendable.

I keep coming back to a simpler line: Man cannot live on bread alone. Something in us still cares about justice and morality even when reality falls short and corruption goes unpunished. You can feel the drain in a brain-dead meeting where bullshit gets treated like wisdom. You can feel the lift when someone speaks truth back into the room.

I took a bet on myself this year to build what was calling to me, even when my business brain said it was not monetizable or career-optimized. AI will only build what we tell it to. If we do not examine the beliefs behind our decisions, we risk accelerating problems we have already failed to face.

Will the people who win under the money-and-status definition finally get what they were looking for? Or will it never satisfy, because that kind of prize never could?
