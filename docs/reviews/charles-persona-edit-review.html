Charles persona editing

This review covers the workflow that changes the production persona. It does not change Charles's current persona or publish anything.

What changes in Studio

Before

The Charles room shows Muxin's original persona brief as a read-only copy surface. The production persona.yaml cannot be reviewed or updated there.

After

The room shows the production YAML in a separate editor. Saving creates an exact old/new proposal. Production remains unchanged until Muxin reviews and approves those exact bytes.

Safety contract

  • No pre-approval mutation: Previewing a proposal never changes production.
  • Exact authority: Digests bind the old YAML, new YAML, proposal payload, and append-only transition chain.
  • Fail closed: Stale, tampered, malformed, schema-invalid, path-injected, or source-stripping proposals are refused.
  • Truthfulness retained: Existing sourced leak-bank entries cannot lose or change their source URL through this workflow.
  • Recovery: Approval is atomic and idempotent. An interruption after the exact rename can recover without applying different bytes.
  • Original brief protected: persona-brief.md stays byte-for-byte unchanged and separately copyable.
  • Delivery unchanged: Charles remains ready-to-paste and manual.

Browser acceptance flow

  1. Edit production YAML and save it for review.
  2. Compare the complete old and new YAML while production remains unchanged.
  3. Approve with review evidence, or reject with a reason.
  4. Reload the room and confirm applied and rejected decisions persist.
  5. The next Charles drafting job reads the approved production persona.yaml.
