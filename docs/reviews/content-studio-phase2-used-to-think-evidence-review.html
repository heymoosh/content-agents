Selection and exclusions

Include
  • Opening presents a belief formerly held by the author.
  • Opening immediately replaces or destabilizes that belief.
  • Source resolves to a captured entry with date and metric provenance.
  • Fixed connective wording is grounded across at least two creator files.
Exclude
  • Generic contrast that does not describe the author’s own changed belief.
  • Later-body reversals that are not part of the opening.
  • Entries without resolvable source provenance.
  • Performance comparisons across creators or unlike metrics.

Originality check

Result: Passed for scaffold use and approved by Muxin.

  • Cross-creator grounding verifies that fixed runs of three or more words occur in at least two cited creator files.
  • A deterministic corpus scan rejects any fixed eight-word run copied from creator material.
  • The frame contains no creator identity, URL, subject claim, or source example.
  • Adaptation boundary: Every belief, fact, example, and phrasing choice must come from Muxin’s own source material. The creator excerpts above are evidence, never writing material.

Proposed pattern summary

Across this bounded three-entry, two-creator sample, writers use a first-person belief reversal to establish the topic and create forward motion in the opening. Because only one of three instances is top-quartile and no controlled comparison exists, the corpus supports testing the scaffold when the source contains a real change of mind. It does not support a claim that the scaffold improves reach.
