I love AI - I have ADHD and I’ve built systems with AI to scaffold the administrative tasks that keep life running but my brain hates doing. I’ve built software solo and using those skills to create a voting tool.

But I’m also wary of the AI powered techno feudalism we’d live in if we did nothing about our legislation or wealth concentration. Arguments about data centers using too much water or electricity are beating around the bush - the real question is, why are these actions legal and sanctioned to begin with?
