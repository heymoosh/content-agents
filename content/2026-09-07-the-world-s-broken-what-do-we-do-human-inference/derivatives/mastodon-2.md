The 1946 Hawai'i sugar strike held 76,000 people together for 79 days on infrastructure, not militancy. Communal kitchens, fishing crews, transport, safety patrols, gardening collectives, alternative banking, coordination across four islands.

The plantation's power ran on workers' material dependency: Company housing, company medical care, company stores. Build the alternative and the dependency breaks.
