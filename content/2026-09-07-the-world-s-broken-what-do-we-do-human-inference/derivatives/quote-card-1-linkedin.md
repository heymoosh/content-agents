Democracy is not only voting and elections. It is the power for people to shape their society according to their wills, and that power requires leverage.

The working test is whether the pressure you can apply costs more to ignore than the incentives keeping things the way they are. If it does not, the effort is ineffective, however sincere it was.

The forms I see most clearly: Our votes, our labor, our spending, our care for one another, and our ability to build new tools and institutions.

This is a primer, not a deep dive. An overview and a map of the territory.
