Organizing only around fear, guilt, anger and opposition eventually exhausts people.

Chile's 1988 campaign against Pinochet used music, humor, celebration, and a positive vision of the future. The jingle did not defeat him by itself. It made opposition feel safe, normal, collective, and desirable.

Joy is not something we wait for after the work is done. It is part of how we sustain the work.
