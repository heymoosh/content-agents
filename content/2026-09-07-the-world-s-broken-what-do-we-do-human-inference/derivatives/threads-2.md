We had an elderly neighbor everyone trusted for free after-school daycare. Tens of thousands of dollars saved across the school years, for families who did not have it. She lived with her family instead of a senior care home, which saved more.

The bigger thing is what happens when you stop filtering every valuable human interaction through money. You empathize more. You come together more.
