We need to stop treating these two groups as if they are the same:

A: Wealthy people/orgs/institutions that are idealistic and want what's best for everyone

B: Wealthy people/orgs/institutions that deliberately shaped laws and policies to entrench their wealth

Group A would not agree with the actions of Group B, even if they benefit from those policies.

This is important, because wealthy people have innate power to influence, and we deserve to work towards a future based on our shared values.
