We can build a future we want - it starts with believing it’s even possible.

Whether AI fully replaces workers or only the repetitive work that can be safely automated remains to be seen: I do not yet see an AI enabled product or workflow that didn’t also require human judgement to set the right goals and tweak the settings. Future models may prove me wrong but as of now, AI practitioners like me see an increasing need for human skills that we didn’t used to care about, like self awareness and emotional understanding.

It suggests to me that the greatest possible unlock to AI’s benefits require more, not less, investment in the broader population. A Universal Basic Investment into education, healthcare, shelter, food - to name a few basic sustaining needs - so that people have the time and energy to develop those human skills, reflect on their experience, and create insights that unlock innovation.

I hope I’m right. But even that vision, which requires humans remain in the loop as we build AI powered systems, does not come by default.
