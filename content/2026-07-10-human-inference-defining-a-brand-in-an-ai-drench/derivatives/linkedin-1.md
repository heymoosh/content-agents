I've been writing on LinkedIn since 2024. Looking back, I can't tell heads from tails what it was actually about. Like most people, I posted because everyone says you should.

I've had a diverse career: journalism, education, anthropology, product, tech, an MBA. There's no industry or professional niche I occupy. I didn't want my next step defined by my past employers either, so "pick your niche" never worked as advice.

For a brand to work, people need to associate you with one thing and stick with it. Over time that repetition becomes the connection people carry around in their head. Every default framing for a brand asked me to pick a lane and cut off a part of the work that felt crucial to keep.

That's why it took most of this year to land on one: Human Inference. Uncovering hidden harmful beliefs before AI automates everything.

I write, build software with AI, and I'm looking to work with people and orgs who share that mission.
