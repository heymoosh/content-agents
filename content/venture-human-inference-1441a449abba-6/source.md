Whenever my mind starts screaming about everything that could go wrong, I picture a tiny rain frog.

It started when I was spending too much time thinking about whether my work would succeed.

Whenever the numbers weren’t moving, I would find another business guru with a bag of magic tricks. Then I’d spend days questioning everything and less time on the writing and building I should have been doing.

I started treating the anxious part of my mind like a tiny frog screaming into the air.

I can let it have its say, pat it on the head, thank it for its input, and do the thing I was going to do anyway.

If your own frog is loud today, give it one minute. Write down what it’s yelling about.

Then set a ten-minute timer and open the thing you already know you care about.

For me, that usually means opening the draft or terminal I’ve been avoiding and working on it until the timer ends. If what’s bothering you is political, [Voter Choice](https://voterchoice.vote/) gives you something equally small: Enter your address, choose one issue, and check one person’s record.

The frog doesn’t have to go away first.

There’s more about how I ended up using this in [Don’t Read if You’d Rather Burn Out Early](https://www.humaninference.ai/essays/dont-read-if-youd-rather-burn-out-early).
