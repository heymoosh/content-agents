Movements that organize only around fear, guilt, anger, and opposition eventually exhaust people.

Chile's 1988 “No” campaign paired opposition with music, humor, celebration, and a positive vision of the future. Participation felt possible and worth joining.

The question for any long effort is what people are running toward.
