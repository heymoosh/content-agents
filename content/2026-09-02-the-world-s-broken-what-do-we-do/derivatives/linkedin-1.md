One of the most useful product ideas in this essay is also the least glamorous: build the thing that helps people use their leverage.

I'm building a voting app because checking a representative's record should be quick and easy. I look at the bills they supported, their stock activity, who funded them, and what they did with the power they were given.

The same logic applies beyond elections. Smaller products can solve real problems without chasing a trillion-dollar exit. AI lowers the cost of making an idea real, but it does not decide which idea is worth making.

The work starts with the system around the tool. Otherwise we make the wrong decisions easier to scale.
