Most people would disagree that they’ll be the ones to experience the expected dramatic rise in living standards with AI.

Most people would also reject the idea that they should bear a one-in-three chance of extinction for a two-in-three chance of much higher living standards. 

This is the inverse of the logic Musk uses to justify a spacefaring civilization: that immense costs are warranted if they reduce the risk of humanity’s eventual extinction. So if species survival is important enough to justify deferring present suffering for a distant future, it can’t also be treated as an acceptable gamble for the sake of AI-driven abundance.
