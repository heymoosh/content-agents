I worry less about AI and more about the humans trying to make decisions on what to do with it. Our biases aren’t obvious to us, what incentivizes us may not be what we actually want to create - yet we have all the pressure to keep up, go as fast as possible, without the clarity that time and reflection brings. 

I have no doubt AI can bring about a lot of good - and bad. Yet the steering is still up to us. And I don’t see enough of the discussion on AI land on challenging assumptions we’ve inherited and ensuring that the things we no longer want does not pass on.
