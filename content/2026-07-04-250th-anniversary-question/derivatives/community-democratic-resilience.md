Vote in the midterms this November, but especially in your primary if your state still has one in season. The primary is when we decide who even runs in the race, and so few people show up that it takes very little to swing the outcome. By the time November comes around, part of the decision has already been made.

This year the entire House of Congress and a third of the Senate are up for election.

I've been building a tool to help people check their rep's actual voting and donation history against what they claim, so you can see whether they've earned your vote: Voter Choice (https://voter-choice.vercel.app/)
