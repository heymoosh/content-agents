Hey Substack - I’m looking for others who feel intellectually lonely in tech.

I often see tech dominated by a certain kind of psyche - engineering optimized, startup growth focused, technocratic assumptions that tech alone solves everything.

If you’re not hitting $XYZ M ARR, growing 50% YoY, getting Sequoia or a16z or Y Combinator level investors - you’re invisible. 

Tech talks big about wanting to change the world but they focus more on getting a lucrative exit, being bought out, and serving their shareholders (though not in those exact words).

Their grand ideas for society are to serve B2B customers or becoming a digital consciousness and interplanetary species.

I love what software and AI can do to solve real problems - make policy, academia, legalese easy for people to digest, take away the boring but necessary administrative work that runs our lives, conduct civilian research into social problems, build tools to help us be more engaged citizens.

But these things don’t make investors happy so they don’t get attention.

I get it - the enterprise business mostly makes more profit sustainably and is less of a gamble compared to consumer products. But one of the things I’ve been looking forward to with AI is the opportunity to tackle niche markets and problems that weren’t profitable before to attempt.

Now with a $200 a month Claude MAX subscription you can create AI systems to build the software, create and distribute content for marketing, and keep you on top of daily life while you’re doing it.

There ought to be more people trying to solve real problems for everyone, not just for the already rich and wealthy.

So yeah, if you could help with that Substack, that’ll be swell 🤞
