I want to pledge something - I will never write an article that points out the problems in society and then leave people feeling hopeless.

Rage baiting based on real injustices is pretty easy - it’s not hard to find real evidence of unfairness and report on it. The algorithm also rewards it. 

But constantly seeing problems without an offered solution just teaches us to be helpless. And we’re not. 

More than ever, we need to be willing to take up agency and steer where our future with AI is headed.
