## Profile

The Diary of a CEO (DOAC) is Steven Bartlett's long-running interview podcast (also on YouTube), now one of the most-listened-to podcasts in the world. Bartlett interviews high-profile founders, scientists, authors, athletes and celebrities in long-form conversations covering business, health, psychology, and periodically AI and society. It is produced through Bartlett's own company (FlightStory) with a dedicated production and guest-booking team, not a small independent show.
