## Profile

Ingested from Muxin's own pre-existing Obsidian vault research (originally gathered for a job search, now repurposed as client-lead evidence). Notion is an all-in-one workspace platform, docs, wikis, databases, project management, and (since 2022) AI, built around flexible "Lego block" primitives rather than single-purpose tools. Founded in 2013 by Ivan Zhao and Simon Last (Akshay Kothari joined as third co-founder/COO in 2018, having sold LinkedIn Pulse for $90M), the company nearly died in 2015-16: it laid off its whole team, scrapped its codebase, and rebuilt from scratch in a rented two-story house in Kyoto before relaunching as Notion 1.0 in March 2016. It grew through power-user community cultivation rather than paid marketing, reached a $10B valuation in 2021, crossed 100M users in 2024, and is now roughly 1,000 employees, $500M+ ARR, $11B valuation, profitable, and reportedly hasn't spent most of the money it's raised. As of early 2026 it is mid-transition from productivity tool to agentic AI platform: custom Notion AI Agents launched in February 2026 (700+ internal agents already deployed alongside roughly 1,000 employees), and CEO Ivan Zhao has publicly and explicitly framed both Notion and the industry at large as still in the early, unsolved "waterwheel phase" of figuring out what an AI-native organization actually looks like.

## Classification

Notion's core company is mature and financially secure ($11B valuation, profitable, no repeated-reopened-role or leadership-churn signal found), so this is not a turnaround read. Per E1-E3, Notion is currently and publicly mid-exploration on a genuinely new, unshipped-at-scale direction: its February 2026 Custom Agents launch and Zhao's own December 2025 essay both frame the agentic-AI-organization question as unsolved, with the COO's real-time posts (E2) showing pricing, reliability, and capability priorities still being set post-launch. The HARD qualifier (open to changing minds) is unusually well evidenced for this corpus: E5 and E7 are two separate, dated instances of Zhao publicly naming an untested assumption that broke a real product, then reversing course once he recognized it, a track record, not abstract philosophy. E8 reinforces this isn't performative: his public reflectiveness extends well past product-execution talk into genuine outside-tech-canon reasoning.

Disconfirmation pass: checked for evidence cutting against "genuinely open, not just executing Ivan's taste." Found two real cautions (E9): Ivan personally reviews nearly every hire and is described, including independently in Muxin's own private notes, as the final arbiter of Notion's product and culture direction, a structure where "openness" could mean openness within one founder's own bounded aesthetic rather than genuine openness to being told he's wrong by an outsider. Notion's hiring bar is also explicitly self-selecting on craft and values, which could mean the org filters for people who already think like Zhao rather than people who would challenge him. Neither rises to a disqualifying "locked roadmap" or "just execute" signal, no such language was found anywhere in the corpus, but both are real structural counterweights to the strong openness evidence above.
