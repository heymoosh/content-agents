## Profile

Ingested from Muxin's own pre-existing Obsidian vault research (originally gathered for a job search, now repurposed as client-lead evidence). Fireflies.ai is an AI meeting-productivity platform built around a calendar bot ("Fred") that joins video calls, transcribes and summarizes them, and increasingly triggers post-meeting workflows (CRM updates, task creation, 200+ department-specific "agentic apps") across 60+ integrations. Founded in 2016 by Krish Ramineni (CEO, ex-Microsoft PM) and Sam Udotong (CTO), it scaled almost entirely through product-led growth to 500,000+ organizations and 75% of the Fortune 500, raised only roughly $19-20M total (Khosla Ventures, Canaan), hit profitability by 2023, and crossed a $1B valuation via a tender offer in June 2025. It runs fully remote (roughly 121 people, 20+ countries), deliberately flat and without a middle-management layer, there is no CPO or VP of Product, and Krish personally makes most product calls. Since 2024 the company has been expanding past its original notetaking core into a broader "AI teammate" platform (agentic apps, a voice agent, WhatsApp integration, a 2026 roadmap for email/Slack handling), positioning against native AI features from Zoom and Microsoft Copilot.

## Classification

Unclear. E1-E3 technically satisfy the greenfield checklist (founder-style hiring for undecided new bets, an explicitly unshipped 2026 product line framed as early-access and feedback-gated). But unlike a clean greenfield case, there's no equivalent to a founder publicly naming and correcting one specific wrong assumption, E6 and E7 are general philosophy statements, not an admission of a concrete reversal. Meanwhile E5's Glassdoor commentary ("all decisions made by the CEO," "autocratic") is a materially stronger counter-signal than typical mild friction: it points at a real single-point-of-persuasion risk on the HARD qualifier, not just imperfect team consultation. E4 confirms no turnaround signal exists at all. The evidence set is genuinely mixed rather than thin, but per the rubric it should not be rounded up to greenfield just because E1-E3 tick boxes.

Disconfirmation pass: searched for a Fireflies-specific "I was wrong" or reversed-decision quote parallel to other leads' founder material; none found in the read sources. Krish's contrarian stories (YC rejection, "why are all AI startups the same") are about being early or right, not about correcting an internal mistake. Also searched for evidence the centralization signal (E5) is overstated; found partial counterweight in E8 (a genuine daily habit of reading customer feedback) and in Muxin's own revised private note (paraphrased: she initially worried "speed" was Fireflies' one value, then softened that concern after reviewing Krish's social media, seeing that outsider/contrarian thinking is valued in the leadership), but neither is a direct quote about changing his mind on Fireflies' own direction, so it softens rather than resolves the open question.
