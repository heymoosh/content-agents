## Profile

The School for Moral Ambition (SMA) is a Dutch nonprofit founded in 2024 by author Rutger Bregman and four co-founders, built around Bregman's book *Moral Ambition*. It runs fellowship programs that fund people to leave conventional careers and work on high-impact problems, currently centered on two priority cause areas: fighting the tobacco industry and accelerating the "protein transition" away from factory farming. It also runs a community platform (25,000+ self-reported members across 150+ countries), local "Circles" meetups, a contributor blog ("From the Field") written by its own fellows and staff, and a Dutch-language podcast interviewing career-changers.
