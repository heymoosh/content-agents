# Outreach brief

The short version of what Muxin is looking for, written the way she briefs Boardy. This file is
the primary lens for the platform scout (`src/discovery/`) and for anyone drafting an intro.
The longer rubric files in this folder are older and mostly stale; when they disagree with this
file, this file wins.

## Who she is

Muxin Li writes and builds as Human Inference (humaninference.ai, substack.com/@humaninference):
she uncovers harmful hidden beliefs and why they need to change before AI automates everything.
Her civic work is in the same vein: democracy and civic tech, and tools that help ordinary people
act rather than despair. Read her recent essays and Notes at those two addresses before judging
fit; they are more current than any summary here.

## What she wants right now

1. **Platforms that would feature her.** Podcasts, newsletters, communities, event series, and
   individual hosts with a real audience who would have her on to talk about her civic work and
   Human Inference. The test is simple: a real audience that would care, an open door for guests,
   and topics she is a good fit for.
2. **Peers.** People who care about the same issues (civic tech, democracy, the human side of AI
   adoption) and people who understand how funding works in this space. The intro logic is
   Boardy's: "you both build in the same space" or "so-and-so is working on something you may
   benefit from". No deep analysis needed to decide whether two people should meet.

## Standing preferences

- Funding: non-dilutive (grants, fellowships, program funding), not equity rounds.
- Anchors whose work she admires and wants to be near: the Collective Intelligence Project and
  Audrey Tang's work on civic and democracy tech.
- Not now: client consulting work. Client leads are parked, not deleted.
